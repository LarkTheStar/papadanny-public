PapaDanny Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

PapaDanny is a private Discord bot used by the management team of a single Discord server to review the quality of the server's support staff. It is not available publicly and is not added to other servers. This policy explains what data PapaDanny processes, why, and how long it is kept.

What data PapaDanny processes

When a support ticket in our server is closed, our ticket bot posts a text transcript of that ticket into a staff-only log channel. PapaDanny reads those transcript files and nothing else. A transcript contains:

  • the messages sent in the ticket by the member who opened it, by support staff, and by the ticket bot;
  • Discord usernames, display names and user IDs of the people in the ticket;
  • the ticket ID, the game the ticket was about, timestamps, and why the ticket was closed.

What PapaDanny does not collect

  • It does not read messages in any channel other than the three staff-only transcript channels.
  • In those channels it only reads transcript files posted by our ticket bot. It ignores every other message.
  • It does not read direct messages, member lists, online status or activity.

How the data is used

Only to help server management review support staff. PapaDanny calculates things like how long members waited for help and how quickly staff replied, flags messages where staff seemed unsure or members thanked them, and produces per-ticket reviews and a monthly audit of each staff member. These are posted only in private, management-only channels. AI scores are treated as a signal for a human reviewer, never as an automatic decision.

The data is not used to make decisions about members who opened tickets, and it is never used to train AI or machine-learning models.

AI processing

To score staff replies for tone, accuracy and professionalism, the conversation from a ticket is sent to Google's Gemini API. Before it is sent, PapaDanny removes Discord user IDs, mentions, links, attachment URLs and activation codes, and labels the member who opened the ticket only as "CUSTOMER". We use a paid Gemini API tier, under which Google does not use submitted data to train its models. Google's handling of this data is described in the Gemini API Terms of Service.

Storage and retention

  • Data is stored in a private database on our bot hosting provider and is accessible only to server management.
  • The full transcript text and quoted messages are automatically deleted 60 days after the ticket was opened.
  • After that, only numbers (such as response times and message counts), AI scores and one-sentence AI review notes remain, so staff performance can be compared over time.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or publish this data. It is shared only with the services needed to run the bot: Discord, our bot hosting provider, and Google's Gemini API as described above.

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete data PapaDanny holds about you by opening a support ticket in our Discord server and asking for the server management team. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through a support ticket in our Discord server.
